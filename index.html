internal tooling · software · security research

Arctor Labs builds and operates internal software-development and security-research tooling for its own infrastructure. Our work covers agent-assisted development workflows, defensive testing, and the operational security of the systems we run.

Research

Isolation and containment: microVMs, unikernels, sandboxed media pipelines, and least-privilege policy design across Linux security modules and namespace-based jailing.

Tooling

Developer environments with verifiable supply chains, deterministic builds, and audit-first automation. We build tools we operate ourselves, on systems we own.

Security

Defensive by charter: hardening, detection, and review of our own attack surface. We do not operate consumer-facing services.
